About Me

I'm a Computer Science student interested in front-end development, UI/UX, and creative design. I enjoy turning ideas into responsive, interactive web experiences that combine functionality with visual appeal and thoughtful details.

My background in Computer Science shapes the way I approach problems, while my creative side pushes me to explore new ideas and look beyond the code to how people experience what I build. I value understanding the purpose behind each project, experimenting with different approaches, and refining the little details that make a difference.

I'm continuously developing my skills, exploring new technologies, and building projects that challenge me to think creatively and grow as a developer.
